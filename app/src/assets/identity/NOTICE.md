# Identity artwork sources

- `crypto/*.png`: [Trust Wallet assets](https://github.com/trustwallet/assets), `master` branch. Native chain icons for BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, XLM, JUNO, KSM and LUNC; Ethereum token icons for USDC and USDT. LSK and HYPE come from NVSTly (below). The Trust Wallet repository's MIT notice applies to its scripts and documentation; token artwork and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
- `flags/*.svg`: [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), `main` branch, `flags/4x3/{us,eu,gb}.svg`. MIT licensed; the upstream copyright and full terms are retained in `FLAG-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`.
- `markets/*.png` and `crypto/{lsk,hype}.png`: [NVSTly icons](https://github.com/nvstly/icons), `main` branch, `ticker_icons/{HPE,DELL,CRWD,ORCL,NFLX,SPX,NDX}.png` and `crypto_icons/{LSK,HYPE}.png`. That repository publishes no license file. Use of these third-party marks outside this prototype should be reviewed with the rightsholders.
- GALA continues to use the existing asset credited in `../coin-logos/NOTICE.md`.

Only artwork used by the prototype is vendored. Fictional assets and tickers unavailable upstream receive category icons rather than an unrelated company's mark.
